Carbonara

Description:

Carbonara is a classic Italian pasta dish celebrated for its creamy, silky sauce made without cream. Originating from Rome, it combines simple ingredients — eggs, hard cheese, cured pork, and black pepper — into a rich and comforting meal that showcases Italian culinary minimalism at its best.

Ingredients:

  • oil
  • guanciale
  • spaghetti
  • 3 eggs
  • cheese
  • seasonings
  • salt
  • black pepper

Steps:

  1. Cook the pork in olive oil until browned and crispy, then drain on paper towels.
  2. Boil the spaghetti in salted water. Drain and return to the pot. Let cool.
  3. Whisk the eggs, 1/2 of the cheese, and some pepper in a bowl until smooth.
  4. Pour the egg mixture over the pasta, stirring quickly, until creamy.
  5. Stir in the pork, then top with the remaining cheese and more black pepper.Stir in the pork, then top with the remaining cheese and more black pepper.
